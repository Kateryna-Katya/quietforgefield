My Little Forge:
Craft & Sell

Run Your Own Dwarven Forge

You'll be digging up ore, melting it down, and forging some pretty epic gear to sell to... well, let's just say you'll meet some really weird customers. There's no rush or anything - just play at whatever pace works for you

FEATURES

  • A Forge That Never Sleeps

    Your helpers will keep working even when you're not playing, so you'll come back to find they've made a bunch of stuff you can sell.

  • Mine, Smelt, Craft, Repeat

    You handle everything from start to finish - raw ore turns into ingots, then weapons, and finally cold hard cash.

  • Customers With Character

    You'll meet all sorts of weird customers, each wanting different things and carrying varying amounts of cash to spend.

  • Grow at Your Own Pace

    Upgrade your forge, unlock new recipes, and expand your workshop without timers or pressure.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Mine Your Materials

    Dig into the mountain to pull out ore and raw resources — the foundation of everything you'll build.

  • Craft & Smelt

    Feed the furnace, work the anvil, and turn raw materials into weapons, armor, and tools worth selling.

  • Sell & Expand

    Haggle with customers, collect coin, and reinvest in better equipment, more helpers, and bigger batches.

ABOUT

My Little Forge is a relaxing business sim from PloxGames — perfect if you've ever dreamed of running your own little dwarven workshop. No pressure here — just there's something really satisfying about hearing hammers clanging on metal and fires crackling in the background. Got 5 minutes? An hour? Doesn't matter - your forge will be ready whenever you are.

Ready to finally build that forge you've been dreaming about?

That ore isn't going to smelt itself, but don't worry - your helpers have got your back.
